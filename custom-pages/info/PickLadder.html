Pick a Ladder

Choose the ladder you want to view and check when it plays next.

The Pick Ladder page

After you sign in, the page lists ladders available to your account. Tap a ladder card to open it. A disabled ladder cannot be opened by regular players.

The top bar shows the app version and the email address currently signed in. Use Log Out only when you need to switch accounts.

Understanding a ladder card

Each card brings together the ladder's name, any image and message supplied by its administrator, and the next scheduled play date.

Optional note for this play date

The date line shows the day, date, how soon play is, and the scheduled start time. If the next date has passed or has not been configured, the card says so. Plan to arrive before the listed start time.

The optional note beneath the date is specific to that upcoming play session.

Other controls

Tip of the Day: If shown above the ladder cards, tap it to read the tip. Choose Next to view another tip or Close to dismiss it.

The help icon opens this guide. The text-size controls let you adjust the app's font size. Super-administrator tools appear only for accounts with that access.
