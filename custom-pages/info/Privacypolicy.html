Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how com.doug.socialsportladder ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares user data when you use our mobile application. By downloading and using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

We collect several types of information to provide, maintain, and improve our social sports ladder ecosystem:

  • Personal Data: While using our App, we may ask you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify you, including your name and email address.
  • Sport & Match Data: We collect user-submitted match results, player rankings, sports scores, and tournament schedules to run the ladder matchmaking features.
  • Usage Data: We automatically collect technical information about how the App is accessed, including device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and IP addresses.

2. Device Permissions & Data Access

To provide functional match check-ins and profile personalization, the App requests the following specific permissions:

  • Location Data: We request access to your device's location. This data is strictly used to verify that you are close to the physical sports courts at check-in time for your scheduled matches. We do not track your location in the background or use it for marketing.
  • Camera & Gallery Access: We require camera access to allow you to take and upload a profile picture.
  • Network/Internet Access: Required to securely sync your match scores, ladder ranks, check-in status, and profile information with our central cloud servers.

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We utilize trusted third-party developer tools to power the application infrastructure:

  • Google Play Services: The App utilizes Google Play Services to function securely. You can review the Google Privacy Policy here.

4. Account and Data Deletion

In full compliance with Google Play's Data Safety Policy, you have absolute control over your personal data. You can request the total permanent deletion of your account and match history at any time:

  • In-App Method: Navigate to the App Settings menu and select "Delete Account".
  • Email Method: Send a request from your registered email address to socialsportladder@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".

Upon receiving your request, all personal data, email records, and account identifiers will be completely and permanently removed from our active databases within 30 days.

5. Security of Data

The security of your data is important to us. We implement standard commercial security measures to protect your personal information. Please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for and does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, we will take immediate steps to delete this information from our servers.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the revised policy on this web page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top of the policy.
